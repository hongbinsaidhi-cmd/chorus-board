Chorus Board Generator

Paste lyrics, generate PowerPoint slides for worship projection

Section headers like "Verse 1", "Chorus", "Bridge" are automatically removed. Paste the full lyrics — the tool handles the rest.

Step 1: Go to a lyrics website (e.g. pnwchords.com) and copy the song lyrics.

Step 2: Click "New Song", type the song title, and paste the lyrics.

Step 3: Click "Add Song to Board". Repeat for more songs.

Step 4: Go to "Song List" and click "Download PowerPoint".

That's it! Open the downloaded .pptx in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

Format: Black background, yellow text (42pt), 2 lines per slide, auto-generated contents page with song list and slide numbers, song title slide before each song.

Tip: You don't need to remove "Verse 1" or "Chorus" labels — they're stripped automatically.
